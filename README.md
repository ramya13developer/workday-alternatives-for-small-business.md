7 Best Workday Alternatives for Small and Growing Businesses

Choosing the right HR software is important for businesses that want to manage employees, attendance, payroll, and daily workforce operations efficiently. Although Workday is a popular enterprise HR platform, its extensive capabilities may not be necessary for every organization.

Many small and medium-sized businesses are exploring Workday alternatives that are easier to manage, flexible, and suitable for their operational requirements.

In this article, we explore seven HR software solutions that businesses can consider when looking for alternatives to Workday.

Why Look for Workday Alternatives?

Workday provides enterprise-level human capital management and financial management solutions. However, some businesses prefer simpler platforms that focus on their specific HR requirements.


7 Best Workday Alternatives

1. BambooHR

BambooHR is an HR management platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses. It helps HR teams organize employee information and manage recruitment, onboarding, and performance.

Key features:

Employee database management.

Recruitment and onboarding.

Time-off management.

Employee performance tracking.

HR reporting.

2. Zoho People

Zoho People offers HR management tools for businesses looking to simplify employee administration and workforce operations.

Key features:

Employee management.

Attendance tracking.

Leave management.

Shift scheduling.

Performance management.

3. Rippling

Rippling combines HR, IT, and finance-related workflows in one platform. It helps businesses manage employee information and automate administrative processes.

Key features:

Employee onboarding.

Payroll management.

Benefits administration.

IT management.

Workflow automation.

4. Deel

Deel is designed for organizations that manage international employees and contractors. It provides tools for global payroll, compliance, and workforce administration.

Key features:

Global payroll.

Contractor management.

International hiring.

Compliance support.

Employee administration.

5. Gusto

Gusto is an HR and payroll platform primarily designed for businesses in the United States.

Key features:

Payroll processing.

Benefits administration.

Employee onboarding.

Time tracking.

HR administration.

6. UKG

UKG provides workforce management and human capital management solutions for organizations of different sizes.

Key features:

Workforce management.

Employee scheduling.

Time and attendance tracking.

Workforce analytics.

HR management.

7. Attendance.ai

Attendance.ai is an attendance and workforce management solution that helps organizations simplify employee time tracking and attendance operations.

It supports businesses that want to reduce manual attendance processes and improve workforce visibility.

Key features:

Biometric attendance using fingerprint and facial recognition.

Face-scan attendance using supported mobile devices.

Employee attendance tracking.

Leave and permission management.

Shift scheduling.

Overtime tracking.

Employee requests and approval workflows.

Centralized attendance reporting.

Businesses looking for dedicated attendance management software can explore Attendance.ai to understand how automated attendance processes can simplify workforce administration.

Final Thoughts

Choosing the right HR platform depends on your business size, budget, and workforce management needs. While Workday offers extensive enterprise capabilities, alternatives such as BambooHR, Zoho People, Rippling, Deel, Gusto, UKG, and Attendance.ai provide different approaches to employee and workforce management.

By comparing features and understanding your operational requirements, you can find a solution that simplifies HR processes and supports business growth.
